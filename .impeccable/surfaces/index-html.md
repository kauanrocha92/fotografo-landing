## Scope

Single landing page (index.html), mode Persuade with Experience leanings: the photographs lead, the action is a quote request (WhatsApp or form). Audience: private individuals in one region choosing a generalist photographer. Proof: the portfolio itself (temporary stock, labelled). Constraints: no invented facts, form has no backend yet, pt-BR, reduced motion respected.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The portfolio composed as a Brazilian concrete poem: photographs and words occupy modules of one rigorous 12-column grid, and empty modules speak too. Refuses the full-bleed-photo-with-centred-serif-name portfolio and the masonry wall.

OWN-WORLD: Flat graphite ink (#0F0F0E) on off-white paper (#F1EFEA), grays for secondary text, one vermilion (#C4391F) rationed to active marks and the primary underline. A faint visible column grid with small module numbers. Heavy tight grotesk words stacked and offset by whole modules; photos are hard-edged rectangles snapped to modules, no rounded corners, no shadows. Dark sections invert the same ink.

STORY: The visitor sees an eye and a method in one glance, browses work by category, sees what can be hired and how it unfolds, and asks for a quote in one tap.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Photo fills the right 8 of 12 modules, full height. Left: three stacked words (luz / gente / tempo) at ~11vw, second word offset one module; a one-line claim below; black block button "Solicitar orçamento" plus underlined "Explorar portfólio"; minimal nav across the top; module index "01 / 12" over the photo.

FORM: Concreto, candidate 6 of 7 on the ordered list; seed:3760bf6487c2740a. Signature interaction: category filters recompose the grid (FLIP slides along modules). Motion grammar: reveals wipe in module steps via clip-path, type rises word by word, parallax only inside photo frames.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
