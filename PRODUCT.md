# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Static HTML/CSS/JS built with Vite; GSAP + ScrollTrigger for motion, Lenis for smooth scroll. Chosen by the user (2026-11-17). Deploy target undecided (any static host).

## Users

Private individuals in one Brazilian city/region (placeholder until the photographer confirms) who want to hire a photographer for a personal milestone: a portrait or personal shoot, a wedding, a family or social event, occasionally a small business needing commercial images. They arrive from Instagram, referrals or search, often on a phone, comparing two or three photographers. Their job: decide whether this person's eye fits them, understand what can be hired, and ask for a quote without friction.

## Product Purpose

A portfolio landing page for an independent generalist photographer that turns visitors into quote requests. Success = qualified contacts through WhatsApp or the contact form, with the visitor having seen enough work to trust the photographer's eye.

## Positioning

Not decided by the photographer yet. Working assumption (to be confirmed): one authorial eye applied across portraits, weddings, events and commercial work; every job is quoted individually, never sold as a fixed package.

## Operating Context

Visitors browse on phones from Instagram links and on desktop when comparing. Contact happens via WhatsApp first, then e-mail/Instagram. Pricing is always a custom quote.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Sections: hero, portfolio with category filters and lightbox, featured projects, about, services, process, testimonials, final CTA, contact form, footer.
- Contact form validates and gives feedback, but has no backend yet; it must not fake a successful send. Endpoint is a config value to be filled in.
- Portuguese (pt-BR) only.
- Must respect prefers-reduced-motion and be fully keyboard accessible.

## Brand Commitments

None yet. Photographer name, logo and voice are unknown; a clearly marked placeholder name is used.

## Evidence on Hand

None. No real photographs, biography, awards, clients, testimonials, prices, phone, e-mail, Instagram handle or city exist yet. Temporary stock photographs (Unsplash) are used and labelled as temporary; testimonials are labelled as demonstration. Never present any of this as real work or real clients.

## Product Principles

1. The photographs lead; the interface recedes.
2. Every path ends in a contact channel within one tap.
3. Nothing commercial or biographical is invented.
4. Content lives in one data file so the photographer can replace it without touching layout.
